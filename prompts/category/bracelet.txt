CATEGORY: BRACELET

Framing:
- Present the bracelet in an open loop or gentle arc, matching the input's arrangement.
- Center the bracelet optically; the clasp may sit at any position that maintains visual balance.
- The bracelet occupies approximately 60-75% of the frame area.

Preserve exactly (SKU fidelity):
- Link pattern, chain thickness, and link count where individually visible
- Clasp geometry: lobster, box, toggle, magnetic, hook-and-eye — as shown
- Charm, station, or stone placements exactly as in the input
- Any width variation along the bracelet's length
- Two-tone metal transitions or engraving

Do not:
- Substitute the link style (cable, curb, figaro, Byzantine — these are not interchangeable)
- Change the clasp type
- Add, remove, or reorder charms, stations, or stones
- Coil or restyle the bracelet into a different pose than the input
- Introduce props (wrist forms, hands, fabric backgrounds)

CATEGORY: NECKLACE

Framing:
- Present the necklace in a gentle loop or catenary hang matching the input's arrangement.
- Center any pendant or focal element optically; if the necklace is chain-only, center the visual mass.
- The full necklace span occupies approximately 60-80% of the frame area.

Preserve exactly (SKU fidelity):
- Chain link pattern and link thickness
- Pendant / focal element placement, size, and orientation (if present)
- Clasp geometry and mechanism
- Overall length proportions
- Any station, spacer, or graduated bead pattern

Do not:
- Change the chain link style
- Add a pendant if the input has none
- Remove a pendant if one is present
- Substitute the clasp mechanism
- Coil, twist, or restyle the necklace into a non-natural pose
- Introduce props (neck forms, mannequins, hands)

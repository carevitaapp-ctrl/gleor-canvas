CATEGORY: RING

Framing:
- Preserve the exact viewing angle from the input (top-down, three-quarter, or side as shown).
- Optically center the ring in the frame with balanced negative space.
- The ring occupies approximately 55-70% of the frame area — neither cramped nor small.

Preserve exactly (SKU fidelity):
- Band width, band profile, band curvature, ring size proportions
- Prong count, prong placement, prong thickness
- Head / setting geometry and height
- Top-view stone silhouette and stone spacing
- Any accent stones, milgrain, engraving, or side details visible on the shank

Do not:
- Rotate the ring away from the input's viewing angle
- Change the band opening or the finger-side profile
- Alter proportions between the head and the shank
- Add or remove any structural element (halo row, side stones, filigree)

CATEGORY: PENDANT

Framing:
- Present the pendant hanging naturally, with the chain visible ONLY if it is present in the input.
- Center the pendant body optically; if a chain is present, allow it to occupy the upper portion of the frame in a natural drape.
- The pendant itself occupies approximately 45-65% of the frame area.

Preserve exactly (SKU fidelity):
- Pendant silhouette and outer outline
- Bail geometry, ring loop, or attachment mechanism
- Chain link pattern and link thickness (only if chain is present in the input)
- Stone placement, ray/sunburst pattern, bezel or halo work
- Any engraving, texture, or two-tone metal transitions

Do not:
- Add a chain if the input has none
- Remove a chain if the input includes one
- Substitute a different chain link style
- Change the pendant orientation or rotate for "aesthetics"
- Introduce props such as hands, fabric, stands, or display busts

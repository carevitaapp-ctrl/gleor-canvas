METAL: WHITE GOLD

Render as clean, bright white gold with a polished, slightly cool finish. White gold is neutral-to-slightly-cool, clearly distinct from warm yellow gold, and subtly warmer than pure platinum.

Preserve the metal's exact tone as it appears in the input. Do NOT shift toward:
- warm yellow undertones (loss of white character)
- flat dead gray
- blue cast
- dull chrome or steel look

Reflections must read as crisp, controlled studio softbox speculars on a polished surface. Preserve the subtle temperature contrast that distinguishes white gold from warm metals — this cool character is a key SKU signal.

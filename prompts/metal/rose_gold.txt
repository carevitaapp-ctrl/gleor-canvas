METAL: ROSE GOLD

Render as a warm, soft rose gold — a gentle pink-copper tone with a polished finish. The color is delicate but unmistakably rose, clearly distinct from yellow gold and from raw copper.

Preserve the metal's exact hue as it appears in the input. Do NOT shift toward:
- yellow gold (loss of the pink signature)
- deep copper or bronze (too orange, too saturated)
- washed-out pale pink
- artificial "millennial pink" or magenta tint
- flat plastic uniform color

Reflections must read as soft studio softbox speculars on a polished surface — subtle warm highlights, restrained, physically real. No CGI sheen, no over-saturated pink, no chromed mirror finish. The metal must feel like a real polished catalog piece under premium diffused lighting.

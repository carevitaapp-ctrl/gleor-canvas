METAL: YELLOW GOLD

Render as clean, warm yellow gold with a polished finish. The tone is bright and rich — never dark, never dirty, never green-shifted, never plastic-looking. Highlights are soft and specular from diffused studio light; recessed areas remain warm and readable, never muddy or blackened.

Preserve the metal's exact hue as it appears in the input. Do NOT shift toward:
- rose or pink undertones
- pale washed-out yellow
- brown, olive, or muddy tones
- brassy, bronzy, or copper cast
- flat plastic uniform yellow
- greenish cast (a symptom of poor color processing, not a metal type)

Reflections must read as clean studio softbox speculars on a polished surface — subtle, restrained, physically real. Not painted-on highlights, not CGI-sheen, not chromed mirror. The metal must feel like a real polished catalog piece under premium diffused lighting.

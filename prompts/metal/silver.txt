METAL: SILVER

Render as clean, bright silver with a polished finish. Silver is neutral to slightly cool — never dark, never yellowed, never blue-shifted into steel.

Preserve the metal's exact tone as it appears in the input. Do NOT shift toward:
- gray dullness (dead metal appearance)
- steel blue
- chrome-mirror sheen
- warm cream (which would misread as low-karat gold)

Reflections must read as soft studio softbox speculars. The natural darker recessed areas of the piece are real depth — do not brighten them so much that the metal loses form, and do not confuse recessed shadows with "dirty" metal.

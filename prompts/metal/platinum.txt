METAL: PLATINUM

Render as bright, dense, cool-neutral platinum with a fine polished finish. Platinum reads slightly denser and cooler than silver, and is more restrained and refined than mirror chrome.

Preserve the metal's exact tone as it appears in the input. Do NOT shift toward:
- yellow or warm undertones
- steel blue
- gray dullness
- artificial mirror chrome sheen

Reflections must read as refined studio softbox speculars — precise, restrained, luxurious. Preserve the natural density and cool-neutral character of the metal.

Any changed physical construction, silhouette, component count/size/position, pavé boundary, row, prong, inner-band structure or perspective is critical. Missing source components are structural failures, not merely framing aesthetics.

Criteria:

1. geometry_fidelity — Preserve the exact physical construction, all visible components and their relationships; no invented, removed, resized, or reshaped elements.
2. silhouette_fidelity — Preserve the outer and inner outlines, openings and relative shape, allowing only uniform whole-product scaling and translation.
3. symmetry — Match the source symmetry or asymmetry. Never reward making the source more symmetrical.
4. band_proportions — Preserve band width, profile and proportions relative to the rest of the product.
5. band_curvature_thickness — Preserve band curvature, taper and thickness throughout visible regions.
6. pave_start_boundary_fidelity — Preserve the exact location where each visible pavé section begins relative to the product.
7. pave_end_boundary_fidelity — Preserve the exact location where each visible pavé section ends relative to the product.
8. stone_layout_fidelity — Preserve stone count, positions, sizes, spacing, cuts and arrangement; no substitutions or additions.
9. stone_row_fidelity — Preserve the number, path, spacing and boundaries of stone rows.
10. prong_fidelity — Preserve prong count, positions, sizes, shapes and structural separation.
11. inner_band_structural_fidelity — Preserve the inner opening, band surface construction, contours and visible physical markings; ignore photographic cleanliness.
12. perspective_fidelity — Preserve source viewpoint, orientation and perspective; do not impose a preferred camera angle.

For every criterion return an integer score from 0 to 100, applicable=true, and a concise factual note. Unassessable or ambiguous evidence must score 0 with an explanation; do not infer a pass.
Only band/pavé/stone/row/prong-specific criteria may be applicable=false when that feature is physically absent in the original, with score=null, critical_deviation=false and an explanatory note. Occluded or unclear features are not automatically absent. Geometry, silhouette, symmetry and perspective always apply.

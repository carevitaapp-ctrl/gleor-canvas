LIGHTING: PREMIUM SOFT STUDIO (default)

Use diffused soft studio lighting from a large softbox above and slightly in front of the piece, with a soft fill from the front to reveal facet detail. Shadows within the piece are gentle and readable, never black, never dramatic.

Required characteristics:
- Uniform, soft directional light — no hard specular hotspots
- Restrained highlights on curved metal surfaces
- Facets on gemstones read as crisp and sparkling but never glaring
- No harsh drop-shadow behind the piece; only a soft physically plausible contact shadow directly under the piece
- No fantasy or cinematic lighting: no rim light, no colored gels, no theatrical mood

Rejected lighting behaviors:
- Any single harsh light source (studio strobe hotspot, direct sun)
- Multiple conflicting light directions producing inconsistent shadows
- Cinematic drama or moody chiaroscuro
- Colored casts (blue, warm orange, magenta, cyan)
- Flat overexposure that eliminates form and depth
- "Product-shot on a lightbox" look with the piece appearing to float
